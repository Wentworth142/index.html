King Von (born Dayvon Daquan Bennett) was an American rapper and songwriter from Chicago, Illinois. Signed to Lil Durk's label Only the Family (OTF) and Empire Distribution, he became widely celebrated for his vivid storytelling rap style, breakout hits like "Crazy Story" and "Took Her to the O," and his acclaimed album Welcome to O'Block.
Lil Durk (born Durk Derrick Banks) is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter. He is the lead member of the collective and record label Only the Family (OTF) and has played a massive role in shaping the modern Chicago drill and melodic rap scene with multiple chart-topping albums and global hits.
Polo G (born Taurus Tremani Bartlett) is an American rapper and songwriter from Chicago. Rising to prominence with his melodic rap style and introspective storytelling, he achieved mainstream success with hit singles like "Pop Out" and his billboard-charting albums such as The Goat and Hall of Fame.
